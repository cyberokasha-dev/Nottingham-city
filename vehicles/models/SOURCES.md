# Vehicle sources

## Licensed everyday vehicles

The sports sedan, luxury SUV and city van are from Kenney's **Car Kit 3.1**, licensed **CC0 1.0 Universal**. `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt` preserves the pack's license. The three original GLB files and their shared `Textures/colormap.png` are included. Their visual wheel meshes are attached to Godot's physical wheels at runtime; body and wheel scale are adjusted together.

- Author: https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit
- Creator-uploaded archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/kenney_car-kit_3.1.zip

## Aurora concept car

`vehicles/vehicle_factory.gd` creates the Aurora model from original procedural geometry: a gold faceted body, panoramic dark canopy, aero wheel covers, front and rear light bars, separate tire geometry, door handles, two seats and a dashboard. Its appearance is inspired by the general Cybercab silhouette. It is not an official Tesla asset or an exact replica; it has no Tesla logo, and its game controls are manual rather than autonomous. No Tesla marketing images, extracted CAD data or restricted model downloads are included. The original generated geometry is covered by the repository's MIT license.

Reference for the design's gold color and two-seat concept: https://www.tesla.com/support/robotaxi/cybercab

## Trucks

The tow rig and articulated truck remain from the official Godot Truck Town demo, under the MIT terms in the root `LICENSE.md`. Their functional tow chain, trailer joints and physics are retained. Metallic surfaces, wheel hubs and headlight details are added by `vehicle_factory.gd`.
