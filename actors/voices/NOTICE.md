# Synthetic dialogue voice credits

The dialogue text is original to Nottingham City. `alba.ogg` and `joe.ogg` contain synthetic speech rendered locally with the MIT-licensed Piper 2023.11.14-2 runtime and the Piper Alba-medium and Joe-medium models. No voice model or runtime binary is redistributed in the game. These recordings do not depict the user speaking.

Piper project: https://github.com/rhasspy/piper
Models: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices

Alba model card is preserved in `ALBA-MODEL-CARD.md`. Its training corpus is the **Alba speech corpus**, Cassia Valentini-Botinhao and Junichi Yamagishi (2019), University of Edinburgh, https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2506 and https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3270. The model card identifies the corpus as CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The game clips are newly synthesized speech using original text, encoded to Ogg Vorbis and slightly pitch-varied per resident. Attribution is retained with these clips.

Joe model card is preserved in `JOE-MODEL-CARD.md`. The model card identifies its dataset as CC0, from https://github.com/OHF-Voice/voice-datasets, and notes fine-tuning from Lessac-medium. Model cards and provenance are retained rather than treating the third-party voices as the repository's original code.

`tools/prepare_voices.py` renders 200 authored phrases in each voice and generates the timing catalog. File hashes are recorded below.

- joe.ogg SHA-256: `80b244216b84b811177d01d208d6f267cacd242cf44276df0d9cfb20aa0408e1`.

Aurora's 18 original scenario lines in `simulation/audio/aurora.ogg` are synthesized with the same credited **en_GB Alba-medium** model and MIT Piper runtime. `tools/prepare_cab_voice.py` and `simulation/cab_lines.json` reproduce them. Three small pitch/text personality variants use this British voice. Aurora retains this Alba voice. Playable characters and residents use the separate British male/female banks described below; Alba and Joe character banks remain for old references.

Engine, electric-motor, bus, door, punch, whoosh, football and goal effects in `simulation/audio/*.wav` are original procedural recordings generated by `tools/prepare_effects.py` under the repository MIT licence.


## Revised character voices

Muhammad, Musa and male residents use Piper **en_GB northern_english_male medium**. Aisha, Jade and female residents use **en_GB cori medium**. Original dialogue is rendered at length_scale 0.92 by the same MIT-licensed Piper runtime. No voice model or runtime is bundled. These are synthetic actors, not recordings or clones of the player's voice.

- Northern English Male model: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_GB/northern_english_male/medium . Dataset: OpenSLR 83, https://www.openslr.org/83/ ; model card states CC BY-SA 4.0 International. Attribution, source and licence are retained in `NORTHERN-ENGLISH-MALE-MODEL-CARD.md`. The generated `northern_english_male.ogg` clips are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Original script and generation tool are supplied; changes comprise synthesis of original dialogue, timing, rate and Ogg encoding.
- Cori model: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_GB/cori/medium . Model card credits Bryce Beattie and public-domain LibriVox recordings; preserved in `CORI-MODEL-CARD.md`.

Only eight of the 32 residents volunteer ambient speech, with an 18–30 second pause between lines. Quiet residents still answer when the player deliberately starts a conversation. Protagonists do not narrate every ordinary action. Combat blocks/hits use effects instead of repeated spoken labels.
